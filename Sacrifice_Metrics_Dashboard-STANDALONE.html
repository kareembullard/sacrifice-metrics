Time Value Calculation

Annual Time Value = (Hrs Saved/Wk × 52) × Hourly Rate

Example: (65 × 52) × $50 = $169,000

Compound Health Benefits

Health ROI = (Health Score Gain × Life Extension Years × Quality of Life Multiplier)

Example: 35 × 5 × $100,000 = $17,500,000

Productivity Multiplier

Enhanced Output = Base Productivity × (1 + Prod. Gain/100)

Example: 100 × (1 + 30/100) = 130 units
